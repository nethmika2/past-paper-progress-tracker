Your 8-month operation, Nov 2026 → Aug 2027

1

Close the syllabus gap first — but don’t stop paper practice

NOV 2026 – DEC 2026 · ~6 weeks

You have ~1/4 of each subject left. Finish it unit-by-unit, highest-yield first (red tags on the Syllabus page). For every unit you finish:

  • Immediately attempt 2015–2025 questions on that unit only — your Deferred Queue “ready now” filter will surface exactly the ones you parked.
  • Mark partial papers (e.g. Paper I Part A only) for covered units.
2

The grind — one paper per subject per week, marked honestly

JAN 2027 – MAR 2027 · ~12 weeks
  • Weekly rotation: week on CM Paper I, week on Physics II, week on Chem I… rotate so every subject gets a paper every ~3 weeks. Recent papers (2019+) reflect the current syllabus — do those first, work backwards to 2011.
  • Time it: 3 h 10 min reading+paper for 3-hour papers, 2 h for MCQs. No peeking — that’s what the “Ran out of time” skip reason is for.
  • Mark with the official scheme the same day. Anything you got wrong or couldn’t start → status Needs redo. Anything untaught → Skipped (Syllabus) + link the unit. Never skip silently.
3

Exam conditions + choice strategy for optional sections

APR 2027 – JUN 2027 · ~13 weeks
  • Full papers back-to-back on weekends, strict timing. MCQs in exactly 2 h — your Paper I speed decides your rank.
  • Learn your Part B/C picks now: CM Paper I/II (5 of 7), Physics Paper II Part B (4 of 6), Chem Paper II Parts B & C (2 of 3 + 2 of 3). Track which option questions you reliably get — park the ones you’d never pick with the “Wouldn’t pick in exam” skip reason and stop feeling guilty about them.
  • Keep a one-page “Silly mistakes” log — most lost marks are unit errors, sign errors and misread questions, not ignorance.
4

The endgame — clear the parked pile, then taper

JUL 2027 – EXAM (Aug 2027) · ~5 weeks
  • Deferred Queue → zero. Sort by “Ready now”, then by frequency-of-topic in 2019–2025. Two subjects a day, two hours each.
  • Last 10 days: no new papers. Only your Needs redo list, formula sheets, and sleep.
  • Practical/lab-based questions (Chem Part A/B, Physics practicals) — revise the set procedures and standard answers cold.

🎯 The 3-bucket skipping policy

  1. Skipped (Syllabus) — the question is fine, you’re just not there yet. Link it to the unit. It unlocks automatically when you finish that unit.
  2. Skipped (Concept) — you don’t understand it even after trying. These are gold: each one is a teaching session with a teacher/video away from marks. Never ignore the pile.
  3. Skipped (Time / Choice) — log it, move on, revisit in July. Keystrokes, not willpower, keep this list alive.

Rule of thumb: a skip is a promise, not a deletion. The queue is the promise-keeper.

📅 Weekly rhythm (repeat 34×)

  • Mon — Finish 1 syllabus unit + log it on the Syllabus page
  • Tue–Thu — Timed past-paper session, one subject per night (~2 h)
  • Fri — Mark that paper with the official scheme; log errors as “Needs redo”
  • Sat morning — 1 hr Deferred Queue sweep (ready items only)
  • Sat night / Sun — Weak-unit rescue: re-teach yourself the unit behind your most-skipped reason
  • Sun night — 5 min: check streak, weekly goal chip, and tomorrow’s target on the Dashboard

📊 How “readiness” is scored here

Per subject: Readiness = 50% × syllabus coverage + 50% × questions marked done.

  • < 25 — Getting started (now)
  • 25–49 — Foundations being built
  • 50–74 — On track for the grind phase
  • 75–89 — Exam capable
  • 90+ — Exam ready

Honest by construction: the app can’t give you marks for questions you haven’t touched. Skipping doesn’t lower it, but neither does it raise it — only doing the work does.
